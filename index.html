PRIVACY POLICY

How Gymate handles your data

Effective 8 August 2026 · Operator: Chow William

1. Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to the Gymate mobile and web application. Chow William is the operator responsible for Gymate. Privacy questions, access or correction requests, and deletion requests can be sent to triboholic@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and authentication: email address, authentication-provider identifiers, sessions, and linked sign-in methods handled through Supabase Auth. A legacy field stores a SHA-256 hash of the verified sign-in identity and is deleted with the account.
  • Profile and age gate: nickname, date of birth, confirmation that you are 18 or older, and room-visibility consent. Status, bio, prompts, photos, and gender are optional.
  • Location and room presence: coordinates used to find, create, enter, verify, and leave gym rooms; room and venue identifiers; visit times; presence state; and the last verification time. Gymate does not store a continuous route.
  • Social and safety activity: Waves, chats and messages, Gymate connections, blocks, contextual reports, Ask-a-Spot activity, notifications, and saved training programs.
  • Device and support: an opaque native-installation hash, push token, notification preferences, app version, platform, and information you enter in a support report.
  • Bonus Waves and referrals: your Bonus Wave balance and grant/spend ledger, personal referral code, one referrer/referee relationship, reward milestones, cooldown expiry, and the distinct eligible account IDs counted toward a new room's founder threshold. Referral records may include the same opaque installation hash used for anti-abuse checks.

3. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate accounts, confirm the 18+ gate, enforce physical room proximity, provide Gymate features, deliver requested notifications, prevent abuse, calculate and protect Bonus Wave rewards, investigate reports, answer support requests, secure accounts, and maintain the service. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Privacy boundaries inside Gymate

  • A one-sided Wave never reveals its sender.
  • A one-sided Add Gymate action never reveals intent or rejection.
  • Grace-period users are invisible to other room members.
  • Bio, full photos, prompts, and quick facts are restricted to Gymates.
  • Blocking works in both directions without notifying the blocked person.
  • Gymate has no read receipts, typing indicators, delivery states, or last-seen status.
  • Ask-a-Spot does not create a social connection.
  • A referral reward never gives the referrer the referee's gym, Wave target, location, or another activity detail.
  • Founder qualification counts distinct eligible visitors privately and never creates a remote member roster.

5. Location

Location is required for Gymate's in-person room features. The app may use background location to check you out after you leave. The backend receives coordinates to validate distance rules. Room creation stores the room anchor; visit records store room-level history and timestamps, not a continuous movement trail. Denying background access means you may need to leave a room manually.

6. Photos, messages, and reports

Profile photos are stored privately and delivered through short-lived authorized links. Messages and closed temporary records may remain server-side for safety and moderation until account deletion even when they are no longer visible in the app. Person reports may contain category, optional description, related-chat context, participants, timestamps, and moderation state.

7. Service providers and transfers

Gymate uses Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, realtime features, and server functions; Expo for application builds and push delivery; and Apple or Google when you choose those sign-in methods. These providers may process data in other countries under their own contractual and security safeguards. Gymate does not automatically import your provider display name, profile photo, contacts, or social graph.

8. Retention and deletion

Most account-linked information is retained while your account exists. In-app account deletion removes the authentication account, profile, photos, messages and connections, visit records, Waves, Bonus Wave balance and ledger, referral relationships, founder qualification entries, blocks, reports, support tickets, programs, push records, and other associated app data. Shared gym-room records may remain without identifying the deleted founder. An opaque installation hash remains without the deleted user ID for five days to enforce the device cooling-off period. Infrastructure logs and protected backups may remain for limited operational periods before rotation.

9. Security

Gymate uses access controls, row-level database rules, private storage, short-lived links, encrypted network transport, server-side eligibility checks, and restricted operator access. No online system is perfectly secure, so please report suspected account or privacy problems promptly.

10. Your choices and rights

You can edit profile information, remove photos, withdraw notification permission, leave rooms, block or unblock people, and delete your account from Me → Delete Account. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by email. We may verify that you control the account before acting. Never send a password or one-time code to support.

11. Age and changes

Gymate is for people aged 18 or older. We may update this policy when the product or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes will be communicated through the app or this page where appropriate.

私隱政策

Gymate 如何處理你的資料

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 8 日 · 營運者：Chow William

1. 適用範圍及聯絡方法

本政策適用於 Gymate 流動及網頁應用程式。Gymate 由 Chow William 營運。如有私隱、查閱、更正或刪除資料要求，請電郵至 triboholic@gmail.com。

2. 我們收集的資料

  • 帳戶及認證：電郵、認證供應商識別資料、登入工作階段及已連接登入方式，並由 Supabase Auth 處理。舊有欄位會儲存已驗證登入身份的 SHA-256 雜湊，並於刪除帳戶時移除。
  • 個人檔案及年齡限制：暱稱、出生日期、年滿 18 歲確認及房間可見度同意。狀態、簡介、Prompts、相片及性別均屬自選。
  • 位置及房間狀態：用於尋找、建立、進入、驗證及離開健身室房間的座標，以及房間、場地、到訪時間、狀態和最後驗證時間。Gymate 不會儲存連續移動路線。
  • 社交及安全活動：揮手、對話及訊息、Gymate 連結、封鎖、舉報、Ask-a-Spot、通知及已儲存訓練計劃。
  • 裝置及支援：不透明的裝置安裝雜湊、推送通知 token、通知偏好、App 版本、平台及你在支援報告輸入的資料。
  • Bonus Wave 及推薦：你的 Bonus Wave 餘額及增減帳目、個人推薦碼、一次推薦人與受推薦人關係、獎勵里程碑、冷靜時間，以及用於新房間創辦人門檻的合資格不同帳戶 ID。推薦紀錄可包括用於防止濫用的同一不透明安裝雜湊。

3. 資料用途

我們使用資料以驗證帳戶、執行 18+ 限制、核實現場距離、提供功能、發送你選擇的通知、計算及保障 Bonus Wave 獎勵、防止濫用、處理舉報及支援、保障帳戶和維持服務。我們不會出售個人資料，亦不會用於第三方廣告。

4. Gymate 內的私隱界線

  • 單方面揮手永遠不會顯示發送者。
  • 單方面 Add Gymate 永遠不會顯示意圖或拒絕。
  • 寬限期用戶不會向其他房間成員顯示。
  • 簡介、完整相片、Prompts 及快速資料只限 Gymates 查看。
  • 封鎖會雙向生效，但不會通知被封鎖的人。
  • 沒有已讀、輸入中、送達或最後上線狀態。
  • Ask-a-Spot 不會建立社交連結。
  • 推薦獎勵不會向推薦人提供受推薦人的健身房、Wave 對象、位置或其他活動詳情。
  • 創辦人資格只會私下計算合資格不同訪客，絕不會建立遠端成員名單。

5. 位置

現場房間功能需要位置資料。App 可使用背景位置在你離開後自動退出房間。伺服器接收座標以執行距離規則；建立房間會儲存房間定位點，而到訪紀錄只會儲存房間層級紀錄及時間，不會儲存連續路線。如拒絕背景位置權限，你可能需要手動離開房間。

6. 相片、訊息及舉報

個人相片會私人儲存，並只透過短期授權連結提供。已關閉的臨時對話即使不再顯示，仍可能為安全及審核保留至帳戶刪除。用戶舉報可包括分類、自選描述、相關對話資料、雙方帳戶、時間及審核狀態。

7. 服務供應商及跨境處理

Gymate 使用 Supabase 提供認證、資料庫、檔案儲存、即時功能及伺服器功能；使用 Expo 提供 App 建置及推送通知；並在你選擇時使用 Apple 或 Google 登入。這些供應商可能按其合約及保安措施在其他地區處理資料。Gymate 不會自動匯入供應商名稱、頭像、通訊錄或社交關係。

8. 保留及刪除

大部分帳戶資料會在帳戶存在期間保留。App 內刪除帳戶會移除認證帳戶、個人檔案、相片、訊息、連結、到訪、揮手、Bonus Wave 餘額及帳目、推薦關係、創辦人資格紀錄、封鎖、舉報、支援票、訓練計劃、推送紀錄及其他相關資料。共用健身室房間可在不再識別已刪除創辦人的情況下保留。不透明的安裝雜湊會在不包含已刪除用戶 ID 的情況下保留五日。受保護備份及基礎設施紀錄可能在有限營運週期後才輪替。

9. 保安

Gymate 使用存取控制、資料庫列層級規則、私人儲存、短期連結、加密網絡傳輸、伺服器資格檢查及受限制營運者權限。任何網上系統都不能保證絕對安全，如懷疑帳戶或私隱問題，請盡快聯絡我們。

10. 你的選擇及權利

你可以編輯資料、移除相片、關閉通知、離開房間、封鎖或解除封鎖，以及在「Me → 刪除帳戶」永久刪除帳戶。你亦可用電郵要求查閱、更正或刪除資料。我們可能先核實你控制該帳戶。切勿向支援人員傳送密碼或一次性驗證碼。
