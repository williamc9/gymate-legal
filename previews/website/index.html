FAQ

The questions you might ask before your first Wave.

Is Gymley a dating app?

Gymley helps adults meet people at their gym. A conversation might become a date, a training friendship, or simply a nicer hello next time.

Can someone tell that I Waved them?

They know a Wave arrived, but not who sent it. Your identity appears only if they Wave you too.

What happens when one of us leaves?

The Waveie conversation ends when either person leaves the gym, unless both people already chose to become Gymates.

Do I need to check in to use training programs?

No. Programs and training tools work even when you are not checked in or meeting anyone.

Does Ask a Spot create a match?

No. It is a short, practical conversation for finding each other and helping with a lift. It creates no Wave or connection.
