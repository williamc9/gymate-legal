PRIVACY POLICY

How Gymley handles your data

Effective 29 September 2026 · Operator: Chow William

1. Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to the Gymley mobile and web application. Chow William is the operator responsible for Gymley. Privacy questions, access or correction requests, and deletion requests can be sent to triboholic@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and authentication: email address, authentication-provider identifiers, sessions, and linked sign-in methods handled through Supabase Auth. A legacy field stores a SHA-256 hash of the verified sign-in identity and is deleted with the account.
  • Profile and age gate: nickname, date of birth, confirmation that you are 18 or older, and room-visibility consent. Status, bio, prompts, photos, and gender are optional.
  • Location and room presence: coordinates used to find, create, enter, verify, and leave gym rooms; room and venue identifiers; visit times; presence state; and the last verification time. Gymley does not store a continuous route.
  • Nearby and live gym figures: when enabled, server-stored room anchors determine whether an active profile and exact checked-in gym room fall within another eligible user's selected 2 km, 10 km, or 20 km distance. Favorite Rooms can show, on request, a banded active-user range, a coarse busy level, and privacy-protected gender percentages rounded to five-point bands when the minimum sample is met. Daily aggregate visit data can provide a peak-period summary. These views do not return exact counts.
  • Favorite Rooms: private links between your account and rooms you choose to save after visiting. Favorites sync across your devices and can be removed in Me.
  • Social and safety activity: Waves, chats and messages, Gymate connections, blocks, contextual reports, Ask-a-Spot activity, notifications, and saved training programs.
  • Device and support: an opaque native-installation hash, push token, notification preferences, app version, platform, and information you enter in a support report.
  • Bonus Waves and referrals: your Bonus Wave balance and grant/spend ledger, personal referral code, one referrer/referee relationship, reward milestones, cooldown expiry, and the distinct eligible account IDs counted toward a new room's founder threshold. Referral records may include the same opaque installation hash used for anti-abuse checks.
  • Wave purchases: Apple and RevenueCat may provide an app-user identifier, product and transaction identifiers, localized price and currency, purchase status, and sandbox or production environment. Gymley stores the minimum fulfillment record needed to credit a purchase once and prevent duplicate credit. Gymley does not receive your full payment-card details.

3. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate accounts, confirm the 18+ gate, enforce physical room proximity, provide Gymley features, deliver requested notifications, prevent abuse, calculate and protect Bonus Wave rewards, investigate reports, answer support requests, calculate Nearby eligibility and live gym figures, verify and fulfill Wave purchases, prevent duplicate or fraudulent credits, secure accounts, and maintain the service. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Privacy boundaries inside Gymley

  • A one-sided Wave never reveals its sender.
  • A one-sided Add Gymate action never reveals intent or rejection.
  • Grace-period users are invisible to other room members.
  • Bio, full photos, prompts, and quick facts are restricted to Gymates.
  • Blocking works in both directions without notifying the blocked person.
  • Gymley has no read receipts, typing indicators, delivery states, or last-seen status.
  • Ask-a-Spot does not create a social connection.
  • A referral reward never gives the referrer the referee's gym, Wave target, location, or another activity detail.
  • Founder qualification counts distinct eligible visitors privately and never creates a remote member roster.
  • Favorite Rooms are visible only to you. They never notify founders, rank rooms, check you in, or contribute to founder rewards.
  • A one-sided Nearby Wave remains anonymous, but its notification names the exact gym room from which it was sent.
  • While you are actively checked in, eligible Nearby users may see your limited profile and exact checked-in gym room. Your device coordinates are not shown.
  • Your selected gender may contribute to privacy-protected percentage bands while you have an active check-in. The split is hidden when the sample is too small and describes Gymley users, not everyone physically in a gym.

5. Location

Location is required for Gymley's in-person room features. The app may use background location to check you out after you leave. The backend receives coordinates to validate distance rules, including Nearby distance tiers. Room creation stores the room anchor; visit records store room-level history and timestamps, not a continuous movement trail. Denying background access means you may need to leave a room manually.

6. Photos, messages, and reports

Profile photos are stored privately and delivered through short-lived authorized links. Messages and closed temporary records may remain server-side for safety and moderation until account deletion even when they are no longer visible in the app. Person reports may contain category, optional description, related-chat context, participants, timestamps, and moderation state.

7. Service providers and transfers

Gymley uses Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, realtime features, and server functions; Expo for application builds and push delivery; Apple or Google when you choose those sign-in methods; Apple for App Store purchases; and RevenueCat to validate and coordinate purchase fulfillment. These providers may process data in other countries under their own contractual and security safeguards. Gymley does not automatically import your provider display name, profile photo, contacts, or social graph.

8. Retention and deletion

Most account-linked information is retained while your account exists. In-app account deletion removes the authentication account, profile, photos, messages and connections, visit records, Favorite Rooms, Waves, Bonus Wave balance and ledger, referral relationships, founder qualification entries, blocks, reports, support tickets, programs, push records, and other associated app data. Shared gym-room records may remain without identifying the deleted founder. An opaque installation hash remains without the deleted user ID for five days to enforce the device cooling-off period. Infrastructure logs and protected backups may remain for limited operational periods before rotation. Gymley deletes its account-linked purchase fulfillment records with the account, but Apple and RevenueCat may retain their own transaction records under their policies and legal obligations.

9. Security

Gymley uses access controls, row-level database rules, private storage, short-lived links, encrypted network transport, server-side eligibility checks, and restricted operator access. No online system is perfectly secure, so please report suspected account or privacy problems promptly.

10. Your choices and rights

You can edit profile information, remove photos, remove Favorite Rooms, withdraw notification permission, leave rooms, block or unblock people, and delete your account from Me → Delete Account. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by email. We may verify that you control the account before acting. Never send a password or one-time code to support.

11. Age and changes

Gymley is for people aged 18 or older. We may update this policy when the product or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes will be communicated through the app or this page where appropriate.

私隱政策

Gymley 如何處理你的資料

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日 · 營運者：Chow William

1. 適用範圍及聯絡方法

本政策適用於 Gymley 流動及網頁應用程式。Gymley 由 Chow William 營運。如有私隱、查閱、更正或刪除資料要求，請電郵至 triboholic@gmail.com。

2. 我們收集的資料

  • 帳戶及認證：電郵、認證供應商識別資料、登入工作階段及已連接登入方式，並由 Supabase Auth 處理。舊有欄位會儲存已驗證登入身份的 SHA-256 雜湊，並於刪除帳戶時移除。
  • 個人檔案及年齡限制：暱稱、出生日期、年滿 18 歲確認及房間可見度同意。狀態、簡介、Prompts、相片及性別均屬自選。
  • 位置及房間狀態：用於尋找、建立、進入、驗證及離開健身室房間的座標，以及房間、場地、到訪時間、狀態和最後驗證時間。Gymley 不會儲存連續移動路線。
  • Nearby 及健身室即時數據：功能啟用後，伺服器儲存嘅房間定位點會用嚟判斷有效個人檔案同確實報到房間，係咪位於另一位合資格用戶所選嘅 2、10 或 20 公里距離內。最愛房間可按要求顯示分段人數範圍、概括繁忙程度，以及只在樣本足夠時以 5% 為單位顯示嘅私隱保護性別比例；每日彙總到訪資料可提供繁忙時段。呢啲畫面唔會傳回確實人數。
  • 最愛房間：你到訪後選擇儲存嘅私人帳戶與房間連結。最愛房間會同步到你嘅裝置，亦可以喺 Me 移除。
  • 社交及安全活動：揮手、對話及訊息、Gymate 連結、封鎖、舉報、Ask-a-Spot、通知及已儲存訓練計劃。
  • 裝置及支援：不透明的裝置安裝雜湊、推送通知 token、通知偏好、App 版本、平台及你在支援報告輸入的資料。
  • Bonus Wave 及推薦：你的 Bonus Wave 餘額及增減帳目、個人推薦碼、一次推薦人與受推薦人關係、獎勵里程碑、冷靜時間，以及用於新房間創辦人門檻的合資格不同帳戶 ID。推薦紀錄可包括用於防止濫用的同一不透明安裝雜湊。
  • Wave 購買：Apple 及 RevenueCat 可能提供 App 用戶識別碼、產品及交易識別碼、本地價格與貨幣、購買狀態，以及沙盒或正式環境資料。Gymley 只儲存用於一次性入帳及防止重複入帳所需嘅最低限度履行紀錄，唔會收到你完整嘅付款卡資料。

3. 資料用途

我們使用資料以驗證帳戶、執行 18+ 限制、核實現場距離、提供功能、發送你選擇的通知、計算及保障 Bonus Wave 獎勵、判斷 Nearby 資格及產生健身室即時數據、驗證及履行 Wave 購買、防止重複或欺詐入帳、處理舉報及支援、保障帳戶和維持服務。我們不會出售個人資料，亦不會用於第三方廣告。

4. Gymley 內的私隱界線

  • 單方面揮手永遠不會顯示發送者。
  • 單方面 Add Gymate 永遠不會顯示意圖或拒絕。
  • 寬限期用戶不會向其他房間成員顯示。
  • 簡介、完整相片、Prompts 及快速資料只限 Gymates 查看。
  • 封鎖會雙向生效，但不會通知被封鎖的人。
  • 沒有已讀、輸入中、送達或最後上線狀態。
  • Ask-a-Spot 不會建立社交連結。
  • 推薦獎勵不會向推薦人提供受推薦人的健身房、Wave 對象、位置或其他活動詳情。
  • 創辦人資格只會私下計算合資格不同訪客，絕不會建立遠端成員名單。
  • 最愛房間只得你睇到，唔會通知創辦人、影響房間排名、自動進入房間或計入創辦人獎勵。
  • 單方面 Nearby Wave 仍然匿名，但通知會寫出揮手發出時嘅確實健身室房間名稱。
  • 你有效報到期間，合資格 Nearby 用戶可能會睇到你嘅有限個人資料同確實報到房間，但唔會睇到你裝置嘅座標。
  • 你所選嘅性別可能會喺有效報到期間加入經私隱保護嘅百分比分段；樣本太少時唔會顯示。數據只代表 Gymley 用戶，唔代表健身室內所有人。

現場房間功能需要位置資料。App 可使用背景位置在你離開後自動退出房間。伺服器接收座標以執行距離規則，包括 Nearby 距離級別；建立房間會儲存房間定位點，而到訪紀錄只會儲存房間層級紀錄及時間，不會儲存連續路線。如拒絕背景位置權限，你可能需要手動離開房間。
